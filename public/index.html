Fair questions

The question stays fixed. The models change. Every answer is published as it was given.

Is this a prediction of what will happen?

No. It is a record of what AI models say when you ask them. The points show where each model puts its weight, not how likely anything is. A model can be confident and wrong.

How exactly were the models asked?

Each one got the same written question with the same 11 futures and had to give whole numbers adding up to 100. No web search and no tools, just what the model already knows. Each model was asked 20 times at its normal settings, and we publish its middle answer along with how much its answers moved.

Why does my chatbot give a different answer when I ask it?

The same model gives slightly different answers each time, which is why we ask 20 times. Your wording, your earlier messages and any web search also change the answer. Each model here was asked once, when it was added, and is not asked again.

Why does every lab count once?

Some labs have many models on the list and some have a few. If every model counted equally, the labs with the most models would set the headline. Averaging inside each lab first gives every lab one equal voice.
