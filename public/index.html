Transparent by design

A benchmark,
not a vibe.

The question stays fixed. The models change. The record is public.

01

One fixed question

Every model receives the same taxonomy of eleven mutually exclusive end states and allocates exactly 100 whole percentage points across them.

02

Closed book

Models answer from internal knowledge and reasoning only. No browsing, no tools, no ranges, no refusals, no hedging outside the number itself.

03

Five samples each

Each model is asked five times at its own default settings. We publish the median allocation and the rationale nearest that median.

04

Automated and dated

Runs are elicited by a scheduled harness that records the exact model id it called, the date, and the prompt version. New releases add to the timeline instead of replacing history.

05

Not scoreable

Unlike a 2030 benchmark, these questions never resolve. The numbers measure a model’s expressed worldview and how it shifts over time—not forecasting skill.

06

Raw data available

Every raw sample, rationale, and aggregate is committed alongside the site, without smoothing away disagreement.
